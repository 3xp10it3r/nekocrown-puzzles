Privacy Policy for NekoCrown: Cozy Cat & Logic

Package ID: com.nekocrown.cozypuzzles  •  Effective Date: November 8, 2026  •  Last Updated: November 8, 2026

NekoCrown: Cozy Cat & Logic ("NekoCrown", "we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-first 3-in-1 logic and block puzzle game (featuring Neko Queens, Neon Block Blast, and Binary Tango) developed for Android and web platforms. This Privacy Policy describes how information is processed when you install and use our App.

1. Local Data Storage (100% Offline-First Gameplay)

We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location to play NekoCrown. All gameplay data—including your puzzle level progress, daily streaks, coin and hint balances, high scores, and unlocked visual themes—is stored locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage (localStorage).

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

The free-to-play version of the App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements. To serve and measure advertisements and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) or AppSet ID.
  • Coarse Location: Approximate location derived from your IP address (country/city level) for regional ad relevance and privacy consent compliance.
  • App Interactions & Diagnostics: Ad views, clicks, crash logs, and basic device performance metrics.

In regions subject to the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices where required before serving personalized ads.

Learn more about how Google processes data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat)

All In-App Purchases (including "Remove Forced Ads Forever", "VIP Founder Pack", "20 Smart Hints Pack", and "Vault of 2,500 Coins") are processed securely by Google Play Billing (and validated via RevenueCat using anonymous purchase tokens). We never collect, view, or store your credit card, debit card, UPI ID, or banking information.

4. Data Retention, Deletion & Opt-Out Controls

  • Deleting Local Game Data: Because all game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, you can permanently erase all App data at any time by uninstalling the App or going to Android Settings → Apps → NekoCrown → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Resetting or Deleting Your Advertising ID: You can opt out of personalized ads or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time under Android Settings → Google → All Services → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

5. Children's Privacy

NekoCrown is a general-audience brain and logic puzzle game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or App features. Any updates will be posted directly at this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data rights under GDPR, CCPA, or India's DPDP Act, please contact us via the developer support email listed on the official NekoCrown: Cozy Cat & Logic Google Play Store listing page.
